## 1. Purpose

This Moderation and Enforcement Policy establishes the principles, procedures, and potential consequences used to address violations of the Next Step Studios Discord server's rules and applicable policies.

The policy is intended to support:

- Consistent and proportionate enforcement.
    
- Protection of Server members.
    
- Protection of the Bot and its supporting systems.
    
- Fair consideration of relevant information.
    
- Responsible handling of reports and evidence.
    
- Appropriate documentation of moderation decisions.
    
- A clear distinction between implemented and planned moderation functionality.
    

The Operator is **Candler Shaw**, operating under the public-facing name **Next Step Studios** (the "Operator").

The Discord server is private and is intended to be the single Server in which the Next Step Studios Bot operates.

This Policy applies to moderation and enforcement relating to the Server. It does not replace Discord's independent platform enforcement.

---

## 2. Scope and Related Rules

Moderation may address conduct that violates:

- Discord Bot Terms of Service.
    
- Acceptable Use Policy.
    
- Community Guidelines.
    
- Server-specific rules and instructions.
    
- Discord's applicable Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
    
- Applicable law.
    

This Policy applies to conduct occurring within the Server and, where relevant to the safety or operation of the Server, conduct outside the Server that materially affects its members or operations.

The Operator will consider the context and relevance of off-platform conduct rather than treating every unrelated personal disagreement as a Server moderation matter.

---

## 3. Moderation Principles

Moderation decisions should be guided by the following principles.

### 3.1 Safety

The safety and reasonable well-being of Server members should be considered when responding to harassment, threats, exploitation, malicious activity, or other harmful conduct.

### 3.2 Consistency

Similar situations should be considered using consistent standards.

Consistency does not mean that every similar-looking incident must receive an identical outcome. Relevant differences in context, intent, severity, impact, evidence, and history may justify different responses.

### 3.3 Proportionality

Where practical, enforcement should be proportionate to the seriousness of the conduct and the risk it creates.

A minor first-time issue may justify a reminder, while serious or repeated misconduct may justify stronger action.

### 3.4 Fair consideration

The Operator should consider relevant evidence and context before making a decision where circumstances permit.

A report or accusation is not automatically proof of a violation.

### 3.5 Accountability

Moderation decisions should be explainable and supported by a reasonable basis.

Where records are maintained, they should be accurate, relevant, and accessible only to individuals with a legitimate administrative need.

### 3.6 Practical judgment

The Operator may take immediate action when necessary to protect members, prevent disruption, preserve evidence, or secure the Bot.

A prior warning is not required in every circumstance.

---

## 4. Who May Enforce the Rules

The Operator retains responsibility for the overall moderation and administration of the Server.

Other individuals may be authorized to perform moderation duties where the Operator has granted them appropriate permissions.

Authorization may be limited by:

- Assigned Discord roles.
    
- Bot permissions.
    
- Specific administrative responsibilities.
    
- Feature-level access controls.
    
- Instructions from the Operator.
    

A staff title does not automatically grant access to every moderation function or private record.

Individuals exercising moderation authority must:

- Act within their assigned permissions.
    
- Use moderation tools for legitimate Server purposes.
    
- Avoid retaliation or personal misuse of authority.
    
- Handle private information responsibly.
    
- Avoid fabricating or altering evidence.
    
- Report significant incidents to the Operator where appropriate.
    
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
    

The Operator may review, restrict, or revoke moderation permissions where necessary.

## 5. Reported Violations

### 5.1 Sources of reports

Potential violations may come to the Operator's attention through:

- Reports from Server members.
    
- Staff observations.
    
- Bot-generated information.
    
- Relevant messages or records.
    
- Support tickets.
    
- Other legitimate sources.
    

The availability of a particular reporting method depends on the Server's current configuration.

### 5.2 Information that may help

A report should include as much relevant information as reasonably available, such as:

- The account involved.
    
- A description of the conduct.
    
- The approximate date and time.
    
- Relevant message links or references.
    
- Screenshots or other supporting material.
    
- Context needed to understand the incident.
    

Do not fabricate evidence or knowingly misrepresent what happened.

### 5.3 Initial review

The Operator or an authorized reviewer may assess:

- Whether the reported conduct falls within the Server's rules.
    
- Whether the available information is relevant.
    
- Whether the report appears credible.
    
- Whether additional context is needed.
    
- Whether immediate protective action is necessary.
    
- Whether the matter should be closed, monitored, or escalated.
    

### 5.4 Incomplete or unsupported reports

A report may be closed or require additional information if there is insufficient information to proceed.

An inability to verify a report does not necessarily mean that the report was intentionally false.

Knowingly fabricated reports, however, may themselves violate the Server's policies.

## 6. Evidence and Review

### 6.1 Relevant evidence

The Operator may consider relevant information available through legitimate means, including:

- Discord messages.
    
- Message links and identifiers.
    
- Screenshots.
    
- User-submitted reports.
    
- Ticket information.
    
- Relevant Bot records.
    
- Statements from involved individuals.
    
- Other information reasonably connected to the incident.
    

### 6.2 Context

Evidence should be considered in context where possible.

A screenshot or isolated message may not show the entire conversation. Reviewers may consider preceding or following messages, the surrounding discussion, and other relevant information.

### 6.3 Evidence integrity

Evidence must not be knowingly fabricated, materially altered to mislead, or presented in a way that deliberately hides important context.

If the authenticity of evidence is uncertain, that uncertainty should be considered.

### 6.4 Private information

Evidence may contain personal or sensitive information.

Access should be limited to individuals who need the information for the moderation matter. Evidence should not be distributed publicly merely because it was submitted to staff.

### 6.5 No guarantee of a formal investigation

Not every report will result in a full investigation.

The Operator may decide that a reminder, direct clarification, or other limited response is sufficient. Serious matters may require a more detailed review.

## 7. Enforcement Actions

Depending on the circumstances, the Operator may use one or more of the following actions.

### 7.1 Informal reminder

An informal reminder may be used to clarify a rule or address a minor issue without treating it as a formal warning.

### 7.2 Formal warning

A formal warning communicates that conduct has been identified as a policy violation or unacceptable behavior and that further violations may result in stronger action.

### 7.3 Content removal or restriction

Content may be removed, hidden, or otherwise restricted where reasonably necessary to address a violation or protect members.

### 7.4 Feature restriction

A User may be restricted from using a particular Bot Feature or workflow where misuse, abuse, or operational risk justifies the restriction.

### 7.5 Timeout or temporary communication restriction

Where available and appropriate, Discord-supported restrictions may be used to temporarily limit a User's ability to communicate or participate.

### 7.6 Kick or removal from the Server

A User may be removed from the Server where continued membership is not appropriate.

A kick does not necessarily prevent the individual from rejoining if the Server's configuration permits it. A separate restriction or ban may be required to prevent re-entry.

### 7.7 Temporary ban

A temporary ban may be used where a period of removal is appropriate.

The duration should be determined based on the circumstances and the Operator's judgment.

### 7.8 Permanent ban

A permanent ban may be used for serious misconduct, repeated violations, substantial safety concerns, malicious activity, or other circumstances where continued membership is not appropriate.

A permanent ban is not required to be preceded by every lesser enforcement measure.

### 7.9 Other reasonable measures

The Operator may use other measures reasonably necessary to protect the Server, members, or Bot, provided they are consistent with applicable law and Discord's requirements.

## 8. Factors Considered When Determining Enforcement

The Operator may consider the following factors:

- Nature of the conduct.
    
- Severity of the violation.
    
- Whether the conduct was intentional.
    
- Whether the conduct was repeated.
    
- The impact on affected members.
    
- The risk of further harm.
    
- Relevant prior moderation history.
    
- Whether the User acknowledged or corrected the behavior.
    
- Whether the User cooperated with a review.
    
- The reliability and completeness of available evidence.
    
- Any relevant mitigating or aggravating circumstances.
    

No single factor automatically determines the outcome in every case.

### 8.1 First-time violations

A first-time minor violation may be addressed with a reminder or warning.

However, serious conduct may justify immediate stronger action even if no prior violation is recorded.

### 8.2 Repeat violations

Repeated violations may justify stronger enforcement, particularly when a User has already received a clear warning or restriction.

The Operator may consider whether the repeated conduct is substantially similar to earlier conduct and whether the User had a reasonable opportunity to understand the rule.

### 8.3 Serious violations

Conduct involving credible threats, targeted harassment, malicious software, attempts to compromise systems, serious privacy violations, or other substantial risks may justify immediate action.

The Operator is not required to issue a warning before taking urgent protective action.

## 9. Warnings and Violation History

Where moderation records are implemented, the Operator may maintain relevant information to support consistent administration.

A record may include:

- The affected User's Discord User ID.
    
- The responsible moderator's Discord User ID.
    
- The type of action.
    
- The reason for the action.
    
- Relevant dates and timestamps.
    
- Supporting evidence references.
    
- The duration or status of a restriction.
    
- Related review or appeal information.
    

The precise record structure and retention schedule must be established before persistent moderation records are treated as operational.

The Bot's persistent moderation and infraction recordkeeping is currently planned and is not confirmed as fully implemented.

This Policy does not promise that the Bot currently maintains a complete or permanent infraction history.

## 10. Conflicts of Interest and Staff Conduct

A person should not use moderation authority to settle personal disputes, retaliate against another member, or obtain an unfair advantage.

Where a moderator is directly involved in an incident or has a significant personal conflict with an involved User, the Operator should consider whether another authorized person should review the matter.

The Operator may review staff moderation decisions and may reverse, modify, or correct an action where appropriate.

Staff members who misuse permissions or disclose private information may themselves be subject to administrative action.

---

## 11. Appeals and Requests for Review

### 11.1 General principle

An appeal is a request to review an eligible moderation decision.

It is not an automatic entitlement to reversal, a guarantee of a response within a particular period, or a guarantee that the original decision will be changed.

### 11.2 Appeal eligibility

The Operator may define which moderation actions are eligible for appeal and what information is required.

The exact eligibility rules, submission limits, review process, and decision authority must be finalized before a dedicated appeal Feature is enabled.

### 11.3 Planned appeal Feature

A dedicated ban appeal workflow is planned and is not currently confirmed as operational.

Until it is implemented and verified, Users should not assume that a Bot command or automated appeal process is available.

### 11.4 Review considerations

Where an appeal process is available, a reviewer may consider:

- The original moderation decision.
    
- The reason recorded for the action.
    
- Relevant evidence.
    
- The appellant's explanation.
    
- Whether relevant context was overlooked.
    
- Whether the original action was proportionate.
    
- Any material new information.
    

### 11.5 Appeal outcomes

Possible outcomes may include:

- Upholding the original decision.
    
- Reducing or modifying the action.
    
- Reversing the action.
    
- Requesting additional information.
    
- Closing the appeal without a change.
    

The final process must be documented before the appeal Feature is treated as operational.

### 11.6 Conflicts of interest

Where practical, an appeal should be reviewed by an individual who can assess the matter without an inappropriate personal conflict.

The Operator may retain final decision authority where necessary, subject to applicable law and the final appeal procedure.

---

## 12. Notifications and Communication

Where reasonably practicable, the Operator or authorized staff may inform an affected User of a moderation action and its general reason.

However:

- Not every action will necessarily result in a detailed explanation.
    
- Urgent protective action may occur before a notification can be sent.
    
- Discord delivery failures may prevent a direct message from being received.
    
- The Operator may limit information to protect other members' privacy or the integrity of a review.
    

A User should not assume that the absence of a direct message means no moderation action occurred.

Where a decision is communicated, the User should use the designated method for questions or eligible review requests rather than repeatedly contacting multiple staff members.

---

## 13. Privacy and Moderation Records

Moderation-related information may include personal information and should be handled carefully.

Access should be limited to the Operator and individuals who need the information for a legitimate moderation or administrative purpose.

Moderation information should not be:

- Publicly disclosed without an appropriate reason.
    
- Used for unrelated purposes.
    
- Shared with unauthorized individuals.
    
- Retained indefinitely without a documented justification.
    
- Altered to misrepresent an incident.
    

The Privacy Policy and Data Retention and Deletion Policy govern the handling and retention of Bot-related records.

Because persistent moderation and appeal recordkeeping are planned, the Operator must define their storage, access, retention, and deletion behavior before enabling them in production.

---

## 14. Abuse of the Moderation Process

The moderation process must not be used to harass, intimidate, or manipulate members.

Prohibited conduct includes:

- Knowingly submitting false reports.
    
- Fabricating evidence.
    
- Threatening reviewers.
    
- Attempting to influence a decision through harassment.
    
- Repeatedly submitting duplicate reports to overwhelm staff.
    
- Impersonating another person in a report or appeal.
    
- Accessing restricted moderation records.
    
- Sharing private moderation information without authorization.
    
- Retaliating against someone for making a good-faith report.
    

A good-faith report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically misconduct.

---

## 15. Urgent Situations

The Operator may take immediate action where reasonably necessary to protect:

- A member's safety.
    
- The privacy of Users.
    
- The integrity of the Server.
    
- The Bot's systems or records.
    
- The availability of critical Server functions.
    
- Evidence relevant to a serious incident.
    

Urgent action may include temporarily restricting access, removing content, limiting a Feature, or banning an account.

Where practical, the Operator should document the reason for urgent action and review whether the measure remains necessary.

---

## 16. Errors and Corrections

Moderation decisions can be made using incomplete or incorrect information.

If a material error is identified, the Operator may correct the record or reconsider the decision.

Corrections should preserve an accurate account of what happened. Where a record must be amended, the Operator should avoid silently changing historical information in a way that creates a misleading record.

The process for correcting or deleting moderation records must follow the Data Retention and Deletion Policy and applicable law.

---

## 17. No Guaranteed Enforcement Outcome

This Policy describes the general approach to moderation. It does not guarantee:

- That every reported violation will be investigated.
    
- That every report will result in action.
    
- That every User will receive a warning before stronger action.
    
- That every decision will be communicated in detail.
    
- That every moderation action will be appealable.
    
- That an appeal will be accepted or successful.
    
- That every moderation record will be retained permanently.
    

The Operator will exercise reasonable judgment based on the available information, the Server's needs, and applicable requirements.

---

## 18. Relationship with Discord Enforcement

The Operator's moderation authority applies to the private Server and Bot-operated workflows.

Discord retains independent authority to enforce its own platform rules.

A Server-level warning, timeout, kick, or ban does not determine whether Discord will take separate action, and a Discord enforcement action does not automatically determine what Server-level action the Operator will take.

Users must continue to comply with Discord's applicable policies.
