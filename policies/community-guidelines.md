## 1. Our Community

Next Step Studios aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and welcoming community.

These Community Guidelines establish the standards expected of individuals participating in the Next Step Studios Discord server (the **"Server"**).

The goal is not to require everyone to think alike, agree on every subject, or interact in exactly the same way. Members may have different personalities, opinions, interests, and communication styles.

However, participation in the Server requires basic respect for other people, reasonable cooperation, and compliance with the Server's rules.

These Guidelines apply to conduct within the Server, including public channels, private support workflows, staff interactions, and other community-related activities.

They should be read alongside the Discord Bot Terms of Service, Acceptable Use Policy, and Moderation and Enforcement Policy.

---

## 2. Respect Other Members

Treat other members with basic courtesy and respect.

You must not use the Server to:

- Harass or intimidate another member.
    
- Threaten physical harm.
    
- Target someone through repeated unwanted contact.
    
- Bully, humiliate, or deliberately provoke another person.
    
- Encourage others to harass a member.
    
- Use discriminatory slurs or targeted hateful conduct.
    
- Repeatedly insult someone after being asked to stop.
    
- Create a hostile environment through sustained personal attacks.
    

Disagreement is allowed. Personal attacks are not required to express disagreement.

If another member asks you to stop a particular interaction, take that request seriously. Continuing unwanted behavior may be treated as harassment depending on the circumstances.

---

## 3. Communicate Constructively

Members are encouraged to communicate clearly and in good faith.

When participating in discussions:

- Address the subject rather than attacking the person.
    
- Avoid deliberately misrepresenting another member's statements.
    
- Do not intentionally derail conversations to provoke arguments.
    
- Give others room to participate.
    
- Avoid repeatedly posting the same message.
    
- Respect reasonable requests from authorized staff.
    
- Be willing to clarify misunderstandings.
    

A disagreement does not automatically constitute misconduct. Context, intent, frequency, and impact may matter when reviewing a concern.

---

## 4. No Harassment, Threats, or Targeted Abuse

The Server does not permit harassment or targeted abuse.

Examples include:

- Repeatedly sending unwanted messages to a member.
    
- Following someone across channels to continue an argument.
    
- Threatening a member or their family.
    
- Encouraging others to attack or intimidate a person.
    
- Sharing private information to embarrass or endanger someone.
    
- Using alternate accounts to continue unwanted contact.
    
- Coordinating harassment outside the Server because of a Server disagreement.
    
- Deliberately exploiting someone's vulnerabilities to distress them.
    

Serious threats, credible safety concerns, or targeted harassment may result in immediate escalation to the Operator.

---

## 5. Privacy and Personal Information

Respect the privacy of every member.

Do not share another person's private information without appropriate authorization.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Home addresses.
    
- Personal telephone numbers.
    
- Private email addresses.
    
- Passwords or authentication information.
    
- Private conversations.
    
- Personal documents.
    
- Financial information.
    
- Private photographs.
    
- Other identifying information that a person has not chosen to make public.
    

Do not attempt to obtain another member's private information through deception, pressure, or unauthorized access.

Do not publish screenshots of private conversations or support interactions in a way that exposes another person's information without a legitimate reason and appropriate authorization.

If you need to report a privacy concern, use an appropriate private support method rather than publicly reposting the information.

---

## 6. Appropriate Use of Channels

Use channels for their intended subjects and purposes.

Members should:

- Read channel descriptions and pinned instructions where available.
    
- Keep discussions reasonably relevant.
    
- Avoid posting unrelated content repeatedly.
    
- Avoid flooding channels with excessive messages.
    
- Use designated support or application workflows when instructed.
    
- Avoid publishing private submissions in public channels.
    
- Follow channel-specific restrictions.
    

Authorized staff may move, remove, or redirect content when necessary to keep the Server organized.

A single accidental off-topic message is not necessarily a serious violation. Repeated disruption after reasonable direction may be treated differently.

---

## 7. Spam and Disruptive Behavior

Do not disrupt the Server through excessive or repetitive activity.

Examples include:

- Flooding channels with repeated messages.
    
- Sending unsolicited mass mentions.
    
- Repeatedly tagging staff without a legitimate reason.
    
- Posting large amounts of meaningless content.
    
- Repeatedly joining and leaving to disrupt the community.
    
- Creating duplicate support requests to overwhelm staff.
    
- Repeatedly posting the same link or advertisement.
    
- Using bots or automated accounts to flood the Server.
    
- Deliberately disrupting voice or text conversations.
    

The Operator may restrict disruptive activity even if individual messages do not appear harmful when considered separately.

---

## 8. Advertising, Promotion, and Unsolicited Solicitation

Do not use the Server to distribute unsolicited advertisements, referral links, promotional campaigns, or unrelated invitations.

Promotion may be permitted only where an authorized channel or explicit permission allows it.

Do not:

- Send unsolicited advertisements through direct messages to members based on Server membership.
    
- Repeatedly promote unrelated communities or services.
    
- Use the Server to recruit members for unrelated projects without permission.
    
- Disguise advertisements as ordinary support requests.
    
- Post deceptive giveaways, referral schemes, or suspicious offers.
    

If you are unsure whether a particular form of promotion is allowed, ask the Operator before posting it.

---

## 9. Content and Links

Content shared in the Server must comply with Discord's rules and applicable law.

Do not post:

- Malware or harmful files.
    
- Phishing links.
    
- Scams or fraudulent offers.
    
- Stolen credentials.
    
- Content intended to compromise another person's device or account.
    
- Non-consensual intimate material.
    
- Private information shared without authorization.
    
- Threats or instructions intended to facilitate harm.
    
- Material that violates applicable Discord policies.
    

Do not disguise harmful links or files to trick members into opening them.

Members should exercise reasonable caution before opening unfamiliar files or links, even when they appear to come from someone they know.

---

## 10. Discrimination and Hate

The Server does not permit targeted hateful conduct or discrimination against individuals or groups.

Members must not use slurs, threats, dehumanizing language, or targeted abuse to attack another person based on protected or personal characteristics.

This does not mean that every disagreement about a sensitive subject is automatically prohibited. Context and conduct matter.

However, disagreement does not excuse harassment, threats, or attempts to make another member feel unsafe or unwelcome through targeted abuse.

---

## 11. Impersonation and Deception

Do not impersonate:

- The Operator.
    
- A staff member.
    
- Another Server member.
    
- A Discord representative.
    
- A third-party organization.
    
- A person or organization in a way intended to mislead members.
    

Do not create a false impression that you have permissions, authority, or approval that you do not possess.

Do not fabricate screenshots, records, messages, or evidence to manipulate a dispute or moderation review.

Clearly distinguish jokes, fictional content, and roleplay from genuine statements where confusion could cause harm.

---

## 12. Respect Staff and Administrative Processes

Authorized staff members help operate and maintain the Server.

Members are expected to:

- Follow reasonable administrative instructions.
    
- Use designated support channels for questions and concerns.
    
- Avoid harassing staff over a decision.
    
- Avoid repeatedly contacting staff after receiving a clear response.
    
- Provide relevant information when reporting a problem.
    
- Avoid interfering with active reviews or administrative workflows.
    

Staff members are also expected to act responsibly and follow applicable Server policies.

A staff role does not give someone permission to harass members, disclose private information, or misuse administrative access.

Concerns about staff conduct may be raised privately with the Operator.

---

## 13. Applications and Staff Conduct

Members participating in an application or staff process must act honestly and professionally.

Applicants should:

- Answer questions truthfully.
    
- Provide relevant information.
    
- Respect reviewers.
    
- Avoid pressuring staff for an outcome.
    
- Avoid sharing another applicant's private information.
    
- Accept that an application may be declined.
    

Staff members and reviewers should:

- Treat applicant information carefully.
    
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure of application details.
    
- Review submissions fairly and responsibly.
    
- Avoid using their position for retaliation or personal gain.
    
- Avoid accessing records outside their responsibilities.
    
- Raise conflicts of interest where relevant.
    

Application and staff records are subject to the Privacy Policy and Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

---

## 14. Tickets, Reports, and Support

Support workflows should be used in good faith.

When opening a ticket or submitting a report:

- Explain the issue clearly.
    
- Provide relevant details.
    
- Avoid unnecessary sensitive information.
    
- Do not fabricate evidence.
    
- Do not use the process to harass another person.
    
- Avoid repeatedly opening duplicate requests.
    
- Respect the individuals handling the request.
    

A report does not automatically establish that the reported conduct occurred. Reports may need to be reviewed before action is taken.

---

## 15. Suggestions and Feedback

Constructive feedback is welcome.

Members may suggest improvements, identify problems, and disagree with decisions respectfully.

Suggestions should:

- Be relevant to the Server or its operation.
    
- Be expressed clearly.
    
- Avoid personal attacks.
    
- Avoid exposing private information.
    
- Avoid duplicate submissions intended to manipulate review.
    
- Respect the fact that suggestions may be declined or deferred.
    

The Operator is not required to implement every suggestion.

---

## 16. Voice and Real-Time Interactions

Where voice channels or other real-time communication Features are available, the same expectations of respect apply.

Do not:

- Deliberately disrupt voice conversations.
    
- Play excessively loud or disruptive audio.
    
- Use voice communication to harass or threaten members.
    
- Record or distribute private conversations without appropriate authorization.
    
- Repeatedly interrupt others to prevent normal participation.
    
- Use soundboards or other tools to create sustained disruption.
    

Members should respect reasonable channel-specific instructions and the privacy of other participants.

---

## 17. Conflicts and Disagreements

Disagreements are a normal part of a community.

Members should attempt to resolve ordinary disagreements respectfully.

If a discussion becomes personal or disruptive:

1. Stop escalating the argument.
    
2. Avoid continuing the dispute across multiple channels.
    
3. Use an appropriate private support method if staff assistance is needed.
    
4. Provide relevant context without exaggeration or fabricated evidence.
    
5. Allow the Operator or authorized staff to review the concern.
    

Do not encourage other members to take sides through harassment, threats, or coordinated attacks.

The Operator may intervene when a disagreement begins to disrupt the Server or harm other members.

---

## 18. Reporting Concerns

If you experience or observe a violation of these Guidelines, you may report it through an available Server support channel or contact the Operator.

**Contact:** [nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com](mailto:nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com)

Where possible, include:

- A description of what happened.
    
- The relevant member or account, if known.
    
- The approximate date and time.
    
- Relevant message references or screenshots.
    
- Any additional context that may help the review.
    

Do not publicly repost private information or sensitive evidence merely to make a report.

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith. Knowingly false reports may themselves violate these Guidelines.

---

## 19. Enforcement

Violations may be addressed under the Moderation and Enforcement Policy.

Depending on the seriousness and circumstances, action may include:

- A reminder.
    
- A warning.
    
- A formal warning.
    
- Removal of content.
    
- A restriction on a particular activity.
    
- A timeout or other Discord-supported restriction.
    
- Removal from the Server.
    
- A temporary or permanent ban.
    

Serious conduct may warrant immediate action without a prior warning.

The Operator may consider the context, intent, severity, impact, relevant history, available evidence, and risk of further harm.

These Guidelines do not establish a guaranteed warning sequence.

---

## 20. Good-Faith Participation

Members are encouraged to participate in good faith.

This includes:

- Giving others a reasonable opportunity to explain themselves.
    
- Avoiding assumptions when context is unclear.
    
- Correcting genuine mistakes where appropriate.
    
- Respecting privacy.
    
- Following reasonable instructions.
    
- Reporting serious concerns responsibly.
    
- Recognizing that staff members and other Users may make mistakes.
    

The Operator may consider good-faith efforts to resolve a problem when reviewing an incident, but good intentions do not automatically excuse serious harm or repeated misconduct.

---

## 21. Changes to These Guidelines

These Guidelines may be revised as the Server develops.

Changes may be made to:

- Clarify expectations.
    
- Address new forms of disruptive conduct.
    
- Reflect changes in Discord's platform or policies.
    
- Improve consistency with the Bot's other policies.
    
- Address safety or operational concerns.
    

Substantive changes should be reflected in the document version and revision date.

Where practicable, the Operator should provide notice of material changes through an appropriate Server channel.
