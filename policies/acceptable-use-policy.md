## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (the **"Policy"**) establishes the rules governing how individuals may interact with and use the Next Step Studios Discord bot (the **"Bot"**).

The Bot is operated by Candler Shaw under the public-facing name **Next Step Studios** (the **"Studio"** or **"Operator"**).

The Bot is intended exclusively for use within one private Next Step Studios Discord server (the **"Server"**). It is designed to support Server administration and community workflows, which may include applications, staff administration, tickets, suggestions, announcements, moderation-related tools, appeals, and other administrative functions.

Not every Feature described in this Policy is currently implemented or available. Some are partially implemented, subject to verification, or planned for future development.

This Policy explains what Users may and may not do when interacting with the Bot. It supplements, but does not replace:

- The Discord Bot Terms of Service.
    
- The Privacy Policy.
    
- The Community Guidelines.
    
- The Moderation and Enforcement Policy.
    
- The Data Retention and Deletion Policy.
    
- Discord's own terms, policies, and rules.
    

---

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to anyone who interacts with the Bot or submits information through a Bot-operated workflow.

It covers use of:

- Commands and slash commands.
    
- Buttons, menus, modals, and other interactive components.
    
- Application forms and application-review workflows.
    
- Staff-related administrative functions.
    
- Ticket and support workflows.
    
- Suggestion submissions and review functions.
    
- Announcement creation and publication functions.
    
- Moderation-related Features, where implemented.
    
- Appeal workflows, when implemented.
    
- Other Bot-operated administrative or community functions.
    

This Policy does not govern Discord's independent services or unrelated websites, applications, games, or software.

## 3. General Acceptable Use Principles

All Users must use the Bot in a way that is lawful, honest, reasonable, and consistent with its intended purpose.

You are expected to:

1. Use each Feature only for its intended purpose.
    
2. Provide accurate information when submitting a formal request or application.
    
3. Respect the privacy of other Users.
    
4. Avoid unnecessary or excessive submissions.
    
5. Follow the instructions presented by the Bot.
    
6. Respect access restrictions and permission requirements.
    
7. Avoid interfering with the Bot's operation.
    
8. Use support and administrative workflows responsibly.
    
9. Follow Discord's applicable requirements.
    
10. Cooperate with reasonable administrative instructions relating to Bot operation.
    

Permission to access one command or Feature does not give you permission to access another Feature or another person's records.

---

## 4. Applications and Formal Submissions

### 4.1 Truthful information

Applications and other formal submissions must contain information that is truthful and accurate to the best of your knowledge.

You must not:

- Invent qualifications, experience, or references.
    
- Deliberately misrepresent your availability or capabilities.
    
- Submit answers copied from another person while claiming they are your own.
    
- Use false information to obtain a staff position or privilege.
    
- Deliberately conceal information that a form specifically and legitimately requires.
    
- Impersonate another applicant.
    

### 4.2 Relevant information only

Provide information that is reasonably relevant to the questions being asked.

Do not include:

- Passwords.
    
- Authentication codes.
    
- Recovery codes.
    
- Payment-card information.
    
- Government identification numbers.
    
- Unnecessary personal information about yourself.
    
- Private information about another individual without a legitimate reason and authorization.
    

### 4.3 Repeated submissions

Do not repeatedly submit the same application or formal request to bypass a review process, create duplicate records, or pressure reviewers.

If you believe a submission failed, check whether it was received before submitting it again. If you cannot determine its status, use an available support method.

### 4.4 Review integrity

Do not attempt to manipulate an application review by:

- Threatening or harassing reviewers.
    
- Offering improper incentives.
    
- Coordinating dishonest responses.
    
- Submitting fabricated evidence.
    
- Using alternate accounts to create misleading impressions.
    
- Attempting to access another applicant's submission.
    

The Operator may reject or restrict submissions that undermine the integrity of the review process.

---

## 5. Tickets and Support Features

### 5.1 Intended purpose

Tickets are intended for legitimate support requests, questions, reports, and other Server-related matters.

Use the ticket system for issues that reasonably require assistance from the Operator or authorized staff.

### 5.2 Prohibited ticket behavior

You must not:

- Open tickets solely to spam or disrupt staff.
    
- Repeatedly open duplicate tickets without a legitimate reason.
    
- Harass, threaten, or insult staff members.
    
- Use tickets to target another User.
    
- Submit knowingly false reports.
    
- Use a ticket to distribute scams, malicious files, or harmful links.
    
- Attempt to obtain private information about another User without authorization.
    
- Deliberately exploit ticket creation, closure, or transcript functions.
    
- Use a ticket to evade a moderation restriction.
    

### 5.3 Appropriate information

Provide enough information for staff to understand the issue.

Avoid sending passwords, authentication codes, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

If a report involves another User, include relevant facts and evidence where appropriate. Do not fabricate evidence or alter material to mislead reviewers.

### 5.4 Ticket privacy

A ticket should not be assumed to be visible only to the person who opened it and the Bot.

Ticket information may be accessible to the Operator and individuals authorized to handle the ticket. The exact transcript and storage behavior is still subject to implementation verification.

---

## 6. Suggestions and Feedback

Where available, the suggestion Feature is intended for constructive feedback and ideas relating to the Server or its operation.

You must not use it to:

- Submit spam.
    
- Repeatedly post substantially identical suggestions to manipulate visibility.
    
- Harass or target another User.
    
- Publish private or identifying information about another person.
    
- Submit threats or unlawful material.
    
- Impersonate another User.
    
- Deliberately disrupt the review process.
    
- Attempt to manipulate suggestion status or administrative records without permission.
    

Suggestions may be accepted, declined, deferred, closed, or otherwise reviewed. Submitting a suggestion does not guarantee implementation.

If a suggestion is already under review, use the existing submission or an appropriate support method rather than creating repeated duplicates.

---

## 7. Announcements and Publication Features

Announcement Features are intended for authorized individuals to prepare, review, and publish Server announcements.

If you are not authorized to create or publish announcements, you must not attempt to access or use those administrative functions.

Authorized Users must:

- Use announcement tools for legitimate Server communications.
    
- Check content for accuracy before publication.
    
- Avoid publishing private User information without authorization.
    
- Avoid publishing misleading or deliberately harmful announcements.
    
- Follow any approval process configured for the Feature.
    
- Avoid repeatedly triggering publication actions.
    
- Avoid attempting to bypass publication permissions.
    

Do not attempt to manipulate announcement history, publication records, or temporary drafts.

---

## 8. Moderation and Administrative Features

Moderation and other administrative functions may be restricted to the Operator or individuals specifically authorized to use them.

You must not:

- Attempt to execute moderation actions without permission.
    
- Access moderation records that are not available to you.
    
- Modify or delete administrative records without authorization.
    
- Impersonate a moderator or administrator.
    
- Use administrative commands to harass or retaliate against another User.
    
- Fabricate moderation evidence.
    
- Interfere with an investigation or review.
    
- Attempt to bypass a restriction imposed by the Operator.
    
- Exploit a software error to obtain administrative access.
    

The existence of a command name, button, or interface does not mean you are authorized to use it.

The Bot's persistent moderation and infraction recordkeeping is planned and must not be treated as a fully operational Feature until verified.

---

## 9. Appeals and Review Requests

If an appeal Feature becomes available, it must be used only for a genuine request to review an eligible moderation decision.

You must not:

- Submit knowingly false information.
    
- Fabricate evidence.
    
- Impersonate the affected User.
    
- Submit an appeal on another person's behalf without authorization.
    
- Use appeals to harass or threaten reviewers.
    
- Repeatedly submit duplicate appeals to pressure staff.
    
- Attempt to manipulate the review process.
    
- Use an appeal to distribute unrelated material.
    

An appeal does not guarantee that a decision will be reversed or reduced.

The dedicated ban appeal workflow is planned and is not currently confirmed as operational. Any future appeal Feature will be subject to its published instructions and the Moderation and Enforcement Policy.

---

## 10. Spam, Automation, and Excessive Use

You must not use the Bot in a manner that disrupts its normal operation or imposes unreasonable demands on its resources.

Prohibited behavior includes:

- Repeatedly invoking commands without a legitimate purpose.
    
- Creating excessive tickets, applications, or suggestions.
    
- Repeatedly clicking buttons or submitting forms to trigger duplicate actions.
    
- Attempting to bypass cooldowns or rate limits.
    
- Using scripts, macros, or automated accounts to abuse Bot Features.
    
- Deliberately generating excessive errors.
    
- Sending malformed or abusive input to disrupt processing.
    
- Attempting to exhaust storage, database capacity, or other resources.
    

This Policy does not prohibit ordinary accessibility tools or reasonable assistive technology. The concern is abusive or disruptive use, not legitimate accommodation.

---

## 11. Security and Technical Restrictions

You must not attempt to compromise, interfere with, or gain unauthorized access to the Bot or its supporting systems.

Prohibited activities include:

- Attempting to obtain the Bot's token, passwords, API keys, or other secrets.
    
- Attempting to access the Operator's computer or local storage.
    
- Attempting to access the Bot's database or internal files.
    
- Exploiting a vulnerability to access restricted information.
    
- Attempting SQL injection, command injection, or other malicious input attacks.
    
- Uploading malware or harmful files through a Bot Feature.
    
- Attempting to crash, disable, or overload the Bot.
    
- Interfering with database integrity.
    
- Tampering with configuration or operational files.
    
- Circumventing authentication or permission checks.
    
- Attempting to extract another User's private records.
    
- Deliberately exploiting an error message to obtain internal information.
    

If you discover a possible security issue, do not exploit it or access information that is not yours.

Report the issue privately to the Operator at:

[**nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com**](mailto:nextstepstudiosofficial.contact@gmail.com)

Include a concise description of the problem and enough technical context to help reproduce it safely. Do not include passwords, tokens, or other authentication secrets.

## 12. Privacy and Personal Information

You must respect the privacy of other Users when using the Bot.

You must not:

- Submit another person's private information without a legitimate reason.
    
- Use the Bot to obtain information about another User without authorization.
    
- Publish private application answers or ticket conversations.
    
- Share private records outside their intended audience.
    
- Use a Bot Feature to harass, intimidate, or expose another person.
    
- Submit sensitive information that the relevant Feature does not require.
    
- Attempt to access records belonging to another User.
    

Do not assume that information submitted to a private workflow is appropriate to redistribute.

The Privacy Policy explains the information the Bot may process and how privacy requests may be submitted.

---

## 13. Files, Links, and Attachments

Where a Feature accepts files, attachments, or links, you are responsible for ensuring that the material is appropriate and that you have the right to submit it.

Do not submit:

- Malware or malicious scripts.
    
- Files intended to compromise another person's device.
    
- Phishing links.
    
- Stolen credentials.
    
- Unlawfully obtained private information.
    
- Material that infringes another person's rights.
    
- Content that violates Discord's policies.
    
- Files designed to exploit the Bot or its supporting systems.
    

The Operator may restrict or remove access to submitted material where reasonably necessary to protect the Server, Users, or Bot.

---

## 14. Circumvention and Evasion

You must not use alternate accounts, deceptive identities, or technical workarounds to evade a restriction or enforcement decision.

Examples include:

- Using another account to submit an application after being restricted from doing so.
    
- Using another account to reopen a prohibited workflow.
    
- Attempting to access a restricted Feature through a different command.
    
- Manipulating identifiers or inputs to bypass permission checks.
    
- Asking another User to perform an action that you are prohibited from performing.
    
- Exploiting a Bot error to bypass a restriction.
    

A legitimate correction, clarification, or authorized administrative action is not automatically circumvention. The context and purpose of the conduct matter.

---

## 15. Respect for Discord's Rules

All use of the Bot must comply with applicable Discord requirements.

You must not use the Bot to facilitate conduct prohibited by Discord's:

- Terms of Service.
    
- Community Guidelines.
    
- Developer Policy, where applicable.
    
- Other applicable policies and platform requirements.
    

The Operator may restrict a Feature or take Server-level action where necessary to respond to a violation or protect the Server.

Discord may independently enforce its own policies. The Operator does not control Discord's independent decisions.

---

## 16. Enforcement

Violations of this Policy may result in action under the Moderation and Enforcement Policy.

Depending on the circumstances, possible responses may include:

- An informal reminder.
    
- A warning.
    
- A formal warning.
    
- Restriction of a specific Feature.
    
- Removal or rejection of a submission.
    
- Temporary restriction from using the Bot.
    
- Referral to the Operator for review.
    
- Server-level moderation action, including removal or banning where appropriate.
    

The response may depend on:

- The seriousness of the conduct.
    
- Whether the conduct was intentional.
    
- The impact on other Users.
    
- The impact on Bot operation or data.
    
- Relevant prior conduct.
    
- The available evidence.
    
- The risk of continued harm.
    

The Operator is not required to use every lesser measure before taking a more serious action where the circumstances justify it.

The exact enforcement process is described in the Moderation and Enforcement Policy.
